⚙ Settings

Sequence engines

Engines define the outreach sequence. Build from a starting point or an empty canvas, run several side by side, assign each lead source its own engine, and share engines as packages — Claude can author one offline and you import it here. Message templates live on the sequence steps: ✏ edit an engine and every manual step, daily blitz, and weekly entry has its own template box ({dm}/{pt} fill per lead) — email steps get a dedicated subject field too. The old Templates tab is legacy fallback only.

Engine assignments by source

Leads whose Source matches a rule use that engine; everyone else uses the default. Match is case-insensitive partial text. 🧪 To A/B test whole sequences: hit ＋ A/B track on a rule and pick a second (or third) engine — new leads from that source rotate evenly across the tracks, every touch is stamped with its engine, and 📊 → 🔀 Sequence tracks shows which approach wins after a few weeks.

Notifications & inbound

Data & analytics

Email & document open tracking

Powered by your Cloudflare tracking worker. When configured, every outgoing email carries an invisible pixel and OneDrive share links route through a counting redirect — opens land in the Comms Log automatically. Opens are a signal, not truth: Apple Mail pre-opens everything, some clients block images.

Deliverability & self-open protection

Bounce & spam-block watch is always on: postmaster delivery-failure reports get matched to leads — the lead gets a ⚠ note, a Comms Log row (bounce / spam-block), and a toast. Replies containing opt-out language ("unsubscribe", "remove me"…) are flagged the same way. True "marked as spam" click reports aren't available to Microsoft 365 senders — server-side spam blocks are the closest real signal.

Self-open filtering needs tracking worker v6: paste solura-track-worker.js (in the dashboard repo) over your worker's code in Cloudflare and Deploy — same URL, token, and storage. It learns your devices from the dashboard's own polling, so opening your own sent emails (or reading replies that quote them) stops counting as lead opens. Filtered self-opens stay visible: the connection test shows them, and the 🩺 report counts them — leads' opens land exactly as before. Reading mail on a network where the dashboard never runs isn't filtered — open the dashboard there once to teach it.

🎙 Voice agent (Retell / Vapi)

Connects the AI phone agent to this dashboard through your voice bridge worker (a second Cloudflare worker, separate from the tracking one — see Voice_Agent_Setup.md). The dashboard pushes each lead's context up so the agent greets callers by name, and pulls every call result back down: outcomes, captured details, booked consults, and opt-outs land on the lead automatically. Unknown inbound callers become new leads. Nothing here dials anyone — outbound calls are triggered by Make.com or the voice platform itself.

How results are applied: a live conversation marks the lead responded (off-sequence, Last Contact stamped) · a booked consult sets status to Assessment Scheduled and puts the visit date in Next Follow-Up · opt-outs join the do-not-call list here and on the worker, which blocks future dials even when the dashboard is closed · every call is written to the Comms Log with its A/B/C script variant, so the analytics hub can compare booked-consult rate per variant. Statuses a human already advanced (assessment / proposal / signed / on service) are never auto-changed.

📰 Newsletter (Resend Broadcasts)

Only the newsletter sends through Resend from hello.solurahomecare.com — every sequence and one-to-one email stays on Outlook exactly as today. Resend hosts the entire unsubscribe flow (headers, page, suppression); opt-outs sync back into column AD and those leads exit ALL automated outreach. Calls relay through your tracking worker (v6.2+), so the Resend API key lives only as a Cloudflare secret named RESEND_API_KEY — add it in the worker's Settings → Variables. Never paste it here.

The newsletter

Written in the same editor as always (variants, PDF, everything) — it just lives here now. When you're ready to start, one button sends it to everyone on the list on the same day, replacing the one-by-one weekly sends. 🧪 A/B testing is automatic: keep 2–3 variants live in the editor and the send splits subscribers between them (stable, even groups), stamping each send's variant — open and reply rates per variant, and the winner, appear in 📊 Batches & A/B. One live variant = a plain broadcast. Until the connection above is configured, nothing sends anywhere.

Who gets the newsletter

Only leads sitting on an enabled sequence position or stage are ever pushed to the list. Default: the long-term nurture phase — the same population the weekly draft has always gone to.



🔥 Warm-up — 14 daily batches, most engaged first

Each day sends that day's batch through hello.solurahomecare.com, reaching further down the engagement ranking as the days pass (sizes roughly double every two days, capped at 90/day). Each send is that lead's weekly newsletter — personalized, tracked, logged, and their sequence stamped. Nobody gets a newsletter twice within 7 days, and leads due their warm-up email this schedule-week are held from the normal weekly send until it arrives.

Contact sync

Pushes eligible leads onto the Resend list (for broadcasts and unsubscribe handling). During warm-up you can sync everyone eligible — the warm-up scheduler controls who actually gets SENT to and when. Free tier: 100/day, 3,000/month.

Advanced — hand-edit a send

The one-button send above does all of this automatically. Use this only when a send needs manual surgery.
